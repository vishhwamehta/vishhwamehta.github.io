Hi! I'm Vishwa — an **Aerospace Engineer** and **Entrepreneur**.

I recently completed my MS in Aerospace Engineering from the [**University of Michigan**](https://umich.edu), where my research spanned **multidisciplinary design optimization (MDO)**,**composite materials** and **aerostructures**.

I'm co-founder and CTO of [**NextGen Diastasi LLP**](/nextgen/), where I've led mechanical design across medical devices, engineering prototypes and miniature architectural replicas. Prior to Michigan, I completed my BTech in Mechanical Engineering at [**NIT Tiruchirappalli**](https://www.nitt.edu).

My research has taken me to [**ISRO**](https://www.isro.gov.in), where I worked on spacecraft thermal systems — specifically flexible heat pipes — resulting in a peer-reviewed publication. I've also contributed to US Air Force-funded research on nanomaterial-reinforced CFRP composites at UofM.

I'm currently exploring roles in aerospace, robotics, and hardware startups at the intersection of structures, systems, and intelligent design.
