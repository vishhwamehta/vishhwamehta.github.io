## Overview

CDR-level conceptual design of a **next-generation strategic heavy-lift airlifter** to replace the aging USAF C-5 Galaxy and C-17 Globemaster III fleet, commissioned as a design brief from the US Department of Defense with an entry-into-service (EIS) date of 2033.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Max Payload | 430,000 lb |
| Range at Max Payload | 2,500 nmi |
| Reduced Payload | 295,000 lb |
| Range at Reduced Payload | 5,000 nmi |
| Ferry Range (no payload) | 8,000 nmi |
| Airport Compatibility | ICAO Class F |
| Regulatory Compliance | FAR Part 25 |

## Design Process

The team followed a full conceptual design sequence from RFP interpretation through CDR-level documentation:

- **Mission analysis** — payload-range trade, fuel fraction estimation, takeoff weight sizing
- **Aerodynamic design** — wing planform, airfoil selection, high-lift system, AVL analysis for lift distribution and stability derivatives
- **Propulsion** — engine selection, thrust-to-weight sizing, nacelle integration
- **Structures** — wing box sizing, fuselage structural concept, materials selection
- **Interior layout** — cargo bay configuration, loading ramp design, multiple payload loading scenarios
- **Performance** — field performance, cruise performance, payload-range diagram

## Vishwa's Contributions

- Interior layout and cargo bay spatial design
- Cargo bay loading configurations (multiple payload types and arrangements)
- Cargo loading ramp geometry and mechanism concept
- Structural contribution to fuselage sizing
