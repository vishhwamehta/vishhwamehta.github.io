## Overview

US Air Force-funded research program targeting enhanced inter-laminar properties of Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer (CFRP) composites through nanomaterial reinforcement — specifically **Aramid Nanofibers (ANF)** and **Cellulose Nanocrystals (CNC)** — to address delamination failures in aerospace structures.

## Goal

Enhance inter-laminar fracture toughness and flexural properties of 16-ply plain-weave CFRP panels by depositing nanomaterials at the ply interfaces during layup, without compromising fiber volume fraction or introducing excessive void content.

## Outcome

- **+10% flexural strength** over baseline 16-ply plain-weave CFRP
- **+8% flexural modulus** over baseline
- Achieved through optimized ANF/CNC deposition technique validated via SEM characterization

## R&D Process

**Nanomaterial synthesis and suspension preparation**
Synthesized ANF and CNC suspensions across 0.05–0.1 wt% concentration range. Dispersion stability optimized through sonication and surfactant selection to ensure uniform fabric infiltration without agglomeration.

**Deposition technique evaluation**
Four deposition methods evaluated on plain-weave carbon fiber fabric:
- Spray coating
- Dip coating
- Vacuum infiltration
- Resin infusion

Uniform nanomaterial distribution validated through SEM imaging at 800–20,000× magnification at the UofM Electron Microbeam Analysis Laboratory (EMAL).

**Design of Experiments (DoE) fabrication campaign**
25+ composite panel variants fabricated via autoclave cure, systematically varying:
- Nanomaterial type (ANF vs. CNC)
- Concentration (0.05, 0.075, 0.1 wt%)
- Deposition method

Fiber volume fraction and void content held consistent across variants via controlled layup and cure cycle.

**Mechanical testing**
- **ASTM D7264** (4-point flexural test) — flexural strength and modulus
- **ASTM D2344** (short beam strength) — inter-laminar shear strength

All testing conducted on Instron Universal Testing Machine (UTM). Results delivered as a validated manufacturing protocol with quantified performance gains for US Air Force evaluation of delamination-resistant composite structures.

## Tools

Autoclave · Instron UTM · SEM (EMAL, UofM) · ASTM D7264 · ASTM D2344 · Design of Experiments
