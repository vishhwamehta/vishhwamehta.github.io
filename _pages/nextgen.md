NextGen Diastasi LLP is a design and rapid prototyping studio co-founded in India, delivering precision mechanical design, additive manufacturing, and product development services to clients across real estate, aerospace, and medical devices. As co-founder and CTO, I have led all mechanical design and manufacturing delivery since the company's inception.

The work spans architectural scale models for India's largest real estate developers, R&D prototypes for ISRO, medical device reverse engineering, and custom hardware for aerospace and robotics clients — all built around the same core: tight tolerances, fast iteration, and manufacturing-ready outputs.

## Featured Projects

---

### [RGB-D Sensor Fusion Fixture](/projects/rgb-d-sensor-fusion/)
*Lead Design Engineer · 2026*

Cost-effective RGB-D sensor fixture for a robotics perception application. Precision housing designed in Fusion 360 with kinematic alignment features, FDM-fabricated and post-processed to achieve ±0.5° angular and ±0.3mm positional tolerance between optical axes. Delivered ±8mm depth accuracy at 1m range with <2 pixel RGB-D alignment error — at <40% the cost of commercial alternatives.

---

### [Ureteral Dilator System](/projects/ureteral-dilator/)
*Lead Design Engineer · 2023*

Reverse engineering of a patent-expired ureteral dilator and sheath system for Meditech Devices Pvt. Ltd., one of Gujarat's leading medical device manufacturers. Delivered 10+ functional component designs accurate to ±25 microns, with full GD&T production drawings (ASME Y14.5) and SLA-printed prototypes in FDA-approved polymers for mass manufacturing readiness.

### Architectural Scale Models
*2022 – Present*

Precision 3D-printed miniature replicas of landmark buildings across Ahmedabad, designed for India's largest real estate developers. Each model is a multi-component assembly — designed in Fusion 360, printed in PLA, and finished for client presentation.

| Project | Client | Components |
|---|---|---|
| **Mondeal One** — Ahmedabad's tallest skyscraper | HN Safal | 20+ |
| **Maruti 360** — twin residential towers | Maruti Group | 100+ |
| **Titanium World Towers** — 2nd tallest under construction | Goyal & Co. | 25+ |
| **Nirma Corporate House** — design presentation model | Nirma Group | 10+ |

Each model required extracting geometry from architectural renders and construction drawings, decomposing into separately printable components with designed-in registration features, and finishing to presentation quality.

### 3D-Printed Heat Pipe Wick Prototypes
*For ISRO · 2023*

Designed and manufactured an array of lattice-structured heat pipe wick prototypes for ISRO's flexible heat pipe R&D program — directly extending the BTech thesis research. Lattice geometries were designed using NTopology and printed via SLA and SLS at micron-level accuracy, delivering functional specimens for capillary performance characterisation at ISRO's Space Applications Centre.
